# Email recall

Approved behavior: checkout abandonment after 30 minutes, paywall-only abandonment after 2 hours, one lifetime recall slot per normalized email and account. Lowest-price enabled public USD one-time credit pack is the paywall default; reserved retention products are excluded. Checkout recipients keep their selected enabled product. Only verified accounts with a paywall or site checkout in the last 24 hours enter automatic selection. Signed links still last 7 days. Spanish account locales receive Spanish; other locales receive English.

## Entry and attribution

The email points to a signed, personal /recall?token=… link (/es/recall for Spanish). GET, SSR, link previews and email scanners do not create payment sessions or checkout events. The owner signs in and clicks Continue to payment after seeing the product, amount and whether it is a subscription. POST /api/payment/checkout accepts recall_token; the server resolves the product and provider, ignoring client product, price, allowance, source and A/B identifiers for recall requests.

Orders persist checkout_source=email_recall and recall_id. Provider metadata includes source and campaign. Recall orders have no A/B participant. Their events are recall_checkout_created and recall_payment_succeeded. The original five-stage funnel is unchanged. Later checkout diagnostics (`checkout_click`, `checkout_signin_redirect`, `checkout_create_failed`, `checkout_redirected`, `checkout_cancel_return`) are separate `funnel_event` rows and are not recall events. Credits, subscriptions, order views and total revenue use existing payment fulfillment. A separate email recall report lives under /admin/funnel.

## Sending and setup

The A white HTML template is fixed in src/lib/email-recall-template.ts, with English/Spanish text, a public HTTPS logo, and a plain-text fallback. It does not offer a bonus. The “most faithful … on the web” claim is marketing copy supplied by the owner, not a verified competitive measurement.

In /admin/settings → Email → Resend configure the API key, verified sender, reply-to inbox, and email_recall_webhook_secret. `email_recall_daily_limit` (default 80, UTC day) and `email_recall_monthly_limit` (default 2500, UTC month) are hard caps. Each send is reserved first so overlapping cron runs share them. `0` sends none. A Resend daily or monthly quota error sets `email_recall_paused_until` to the next UTC midnight and pauses the run. The From display name is admin `app_name` (fallback `VITE_APP_NAME` or `Uptown`) unless the sender address already includes one. Set up /api/email-recall/webhook in Resend for email.bounced and email.complained. Signatures/timestamps are verified. Unsubscribed, bounced and complained addresses are excluded. Enable only for contacts you are permitted to email.

Nitro runs email-recall every five minutes. Cloudflare Cron Triggers are generated by pnpm cf:build; Node uses Nitro's scheduler while running. Automatic sending defaults off. Building does not send messages. The admin report can issue a personal link without making a payment session. Issued but unsent links share the lifetime recipient slot.

The send owner is claimed with database compare-and-set; requests use a stable Resend idempotency key. Provider acceptance consumes the slot; an error/timeout becomes uncertain and is never automatically retried, including beyond Resend's 24-hour idempotency window. A process interrupted after claiming may remain sending; review it manually rather than blindly retrying.

Signed unsubscribe capabilities cannot be used for checkout and remain usable after checkout-link expiry.

## Contact support

Use [support tickets](/settings/tickets) to reach the team. Sign in when prompted, open a ticket, and follow the replies in your account. This page points at that channel. It does not promise a separate inbox or a response time.

## What to include

Say what you tried to generate, the prompt in general terms, the browser, and the error you saw. Do not attach someone else's private photos.

## Privacy requests

Use the same tickets for privacy requests. Include the page URL and a short description. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).

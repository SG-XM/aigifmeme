## What this product does

AIGifMeme is an AI meme and sticker GIF generator. You describe a joke, a character, or a reaction. The product is for still meme stickers and short looping GIF memes you can drop into a chat.

Examples on the homepage are placeholders that show the kind of result: a reaction sticker, a caption loop, a pet meme, and a poster sticker. Generating and saving a result uses an account and credits.

## What it does not do

AIGifMeme does not remove metadata from PDF, Office, or image files. It is not a document cleaner, and it is not a music-video or dance-video campaign.
